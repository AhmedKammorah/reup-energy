How ReUP works

A full-stack mobile marine power service — hardware, software, and humans on the dock.

01

Request

Captain or marina opens the ReUP app, picks a berth, sets a kWh target and deadline.

02

Dispatch

Our mobile power unit is wheeled, towed, or floated to the berth by a ReUP operator.

03

Charge

Plug in via marine-grade connectors. Live telemetry in-app. Auto-bill on completion.
